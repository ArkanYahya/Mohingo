# 0006: More than one level — a current level, earlier-level references, and level locks

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The app was built for A1 alone. An A2 curriculum (from `reference/a2-modular-course/`) is being adapted to continue it — see docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-19-a2-level-design.md. A2 has to feel like the next stage of the same course: its lessons review A1 language, a learner's A1 words keep coming up for review while they study A2, and a learner who already knows A1 must be able to start A2 without being blocked. The A2 reference also re-registered 30 A1 words and several A1 grammar points under new A2 ids, which breaks the curriculum's own rule ("reuse the id, never copy the record").

## Decision

1. **Levels are one journey with a current level.** The learner works in one current level (stored with their progress, so "Reset progress" returns them to A1). Home and Tests follow it; Review is shared across levels; Vocabulary and Grammar open on it with a filter.
2. **A level may build on an earlier one.** The app-owned catalogue declares `prerequisiteLevelId` (A2 → A1). The earlier level loads first and the later level's index is built with it attached, so a lesson may list the earlier level's grammar, word and phrase ids — shown to learners as "From A1". Bundles stay self-contained files; the dependency lives in the catalogue.
3. **Items open in their own level.** Every bundle id starts with its level (`A1-U3`, `A2.LEX…`); a screen that opens one item finds the level from the id.
4. **Levels lock softly.** Before a level is released it is a locked preview (titles and topics browsable, lessons closed). After release A2 opens once A1 is complete, after a placement result in A2, or when the learner chooses "Open A2 anyway". Units within a level stay recommendation-only.

## Alternatives considered

- **Separate courses per level** (switching level switches the whole app, Review included) — rejected: A1 words would stop being reviewed exactly when A2 starts relying on them.
- **Strict order (A2 only after A1 is finished)** — rejected: contradicts "recommend, never lock", and would block adults who already know A1.
- **Copying A1 records into the A2 bundle** — rejected: two ids for one sense, two review schedules for one word.
- **One merged bundle for all levels** — rejected: larger startup cost and every level's edits in one file; the catalogue already makes levels additive.

## Consequences

- A2's index depends on A1's: if A1 fails to load, A2 does too (A2 failing never takes A1 down).
- Ids must keep their level prefix; a test fixture without one can't be opened by id.
- Later levels (B1+) reuse the same mechanism: each declares the level it builds on.
- **2026-09-20, decision 2 narrowed by the product owner:** a level still declares the level it builds on, and the content layer still loads and indexes that earlier level alongside it — but **A2 lessons do not reference A1 ids**. A learner in A2 is assumed to know A1, whether they finished it here or arrived already knowing it, so A2 teaches A2 and its bundle stays self-contained. The alternative gave every learner who completed A1 a second spaced-review row for words they already knew, to benefit only the learner who taps "Open anyway" without doing A1. Consequence, recorded deliberately rather than discovered later: `CourseContentIndex.build`'s `prerequisite` parameter, `courseContentProvider` loading A1 alongside A2, and the key-phrases screen's "From A1" badge currently have **no consumer**. They are kept — unused — because grammar may still want them and B1 will face the same question. See the A2 design spec's Design 1.
- Landed so far: decision 2 (a level building on an earlier one) and decision 3 (items open in their own level), from step 1 (`feature/a2-foundations`). Decision 4 (levels lock softly) is now in code, and decision 1 (a current level) is in code for the Learn tab — the level a learner is enrolled in drives its switcher, its lock sheet, and where "you are here" resolves — both from step 2a (`feature/a2-current-level`). Decision 1's remaining tabs are step 2b: the Tests tab, both capstones and Home landed (`feature/a2-pilot-u1`, via `currentCourseProvider`) — Home also carrying the one *change*-of-level affordance decision 4 implies, an offer of the next level once this one is mastered, which the learner takes rather than the app — and Words & phrases and Grammar landed with them, with the filter decision 1 names (`LevelFilterChips`), which shows another level's registry without ever making it current. The Review tab has not, and the placement test is deliberately left — by design it covers every released level, which is its own task.
- **2026-09-21 (`feature/a2-pilot-u1`): A2 is released.** Its content was authored and `kLevelCatalog` marks it `available`, so decision 4's second half is live for the first time — A2 reads `open` for a learner who has mastered every counting A1 unit or has tapped "Open A2 anyway", `lockedNeedsPrerequisite` otherwise, and no level is a `preview` any more. Two consequences worth stating rather than discovering:
  - The 2026-09-20 narrowing above is now measured, not predicted: **0** of A2's 84 lessons reference an A1 id. `CourseContentIndex.build`'s `prerequisite`, `courseContentProvider` loading A1 alongside A2, and the "From A1" badge are therefore dead paths in production — still tested, still correct, still there for grammar and for B1, but exercised only by fixtures. A reader who finds them and assumes A2 uses them will be wrong.
  - Decision 1 shipped half-done into a released level, which is a different thing from shipping half-done into a preview one. Everything outside the Learn tab still read `'a1'` by name, so a learner who moved to A2 saw A1's Home card, A1's words, A1's grammar and A1's tests, with copy that said "A1" in twelve places — and A2's own capstones, gated but unreachable. Step 2b was no longer preparatory work; it was the gap between what the catalogue claims and what the app does. **Closed for the exit path the same day:** the Tests tab, the Final Mission and the Final Assessment now follow `currentCourseProvider`, so an A2 learner can finish A2. **Closed for the entry path the same day too:** Home follows it as well, so an A2 learner opens the app to A2 — and a learner who has mastered every unit of their level is offered the next one instead of being told they have finished everything. **And for the two reference lists the same day:** Words & phrases and Grammar open on the current level with the A1/A2 filter, so A2's 305 senses, 145 phrases and 74 forms are reachable and A1's are one chip away — filtering is a view, never a `select`, because an A2 learner is still reviewing A1's words. The Review tab is still on A1, and five of the twelve strings still name it. See [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) and [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md)'s "What follows the current level, and what doesn't".
