Mohingo · Design pass 3

A grounded card & feedback system, not another single-colour patch

The last two passes swapped one colorScheme role at a time, which is exactly why some things went vivid and others stayed muted. This pass covers cards, answer states, status icons, and both light/dark mode together, and every decision below is grounded in a real, named source rather than my own taste:

schoolDuolingo's own colour-meaning system (green=correct, red=wrong, one accent per state) contrastWCAG 2.2 §1.4.11 — 3:1 minimum for buttons/chips, not just text dark_modeMaterial 3's dark-theme guidance — tonal containers over saturated fills on near-black grid_viewMaterial Symbols Rounded — one real icon set instead of hand-drawn shapes
check_circleCorrect / incorrect

A universal green / red, not your theme's brand colour — that's why "correct" read as an odd orange on Warm Terracotta before.

dashboardHero card

"Continue learning" is now a confident solid brand-colour card with an inverted white button — no more same-hue-on-same-hue.

dark_modeDark mode

Feedback banners switch to tinted containers with bright text instead of solid fills — a saturated fill on near-black glows/vibrates.

progress_activityStatus icons

In progress gets its own warm gold, distinct from "mastered" (green) and your brand colour — three states, three reads.
