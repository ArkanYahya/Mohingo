Mohingo · Colour theme review

Why the app reads muted, and what a punchier accent looks like

Every colour role is generated from one seed colour via Material 3's ColorScheme.fromSeed(), and the seeds themselves were chosen deliberately muted — calm over energetic. Below is each of the 3 selectable themes today, next to one proposed change: keep every muted background exactly as it is, but swap colorScheme.primary — the one role that drives buttons, selected answers, progress bars and the “correct” state — for a flat, saturated brand tone.

How these were chosen, not just picked by eye: each proposed colour was swept across lightness at full saturation and checked against WCAG 2.1 contrast with its on-colour — the same 4.5:1 floor docs/UX_PRINCIPLES.md already holds streakAccent to. Everything shown here clears it.

What doesn't change: backgrounds, cards, secondary/tertiary roles, error red, and the streak accent all stay exactly as they are — this only touches the one role behind buttons, selected answers, progress fills and the “correct” state. Dark mode gets the same treatment (each theme's own M3-generated dark primary/onPrimary swapped the same way).

Reply with which theme(s) you want shipped — all three, just the default, or a mix — and it's a small, contained change to lib/core/theme/app_theme.dart and color_seed.dart.
